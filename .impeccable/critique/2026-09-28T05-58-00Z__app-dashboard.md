## Design Specificity Verdict
Split. The phone frame (config-driven top bar, role-tuned tab bar, menu sheet), HomeKit homes, attendance register, Record Payment and Library Desk are authored for this product. About 70% of page bodies are desktop layouts collapsed with flex-col: eyebrow labels, stat-tile rows (often all zeros), icon-in-tinted-square metric cards, three "Welcome, School" heroes. /cbt is a marketing landing page with invented proof.

Deterministic scan: 16 findings (app/dashboard 13, app/cbt 3). 14 gray-on-color are false positives (hover-state pairs). 2 true design-system-color hits (#cbd5e1 at results/page.tsx:195 and app/cbt/page.tsx:95). Detector missed Tailwind-default hexes in components/dashboard/student-academic-record.tsx:73-84. Browser: 0 page-level overflow on 16 pages; one sideways-scrolling <main> on teachers/[id] (392 vs 375). No Tailwind-default navy slate anywhere (palette lock in globals.css works). Overlay on /cbt: 11 anti-patterns (nested cards x4, 10px text, low contrast 2.5:1).

## Priority Issues
- [P0] Score entry is hostile on a phone: 56x36 inputs at 14px (iOS zooms each), no inputMode, max score scrolls away, storage order not alphabetical, unsaved state computed but hidden. Fix: 16px 48px inputs with /max suffix, sticky header with entered count, alphabetical order, dirty count + leave guard, safe-area save bar.
- [P0] /cbt signed-in "Sign Out" calls the HCBT dev-logout endpoint; the page shows invented proof ("Best Certified Teacher Worldwide", "210+ Experts"). Fix: real logout; delete invented proof.
- [P1] Two titles / two H1s on almost every page, often disagreeing (Payments vs Payment Settings, Spreadsheets vs Welcome, School). Fix: top bar is the only phone title; pages push entity names into it and hide their own H1 below md.
- [P1] Pages override phone-sized primitives smaller: 53 Button h-7/8/9 overrides, 41 inputs at text-sm/xs (iOS zoom), tabs at 41px. Fix: primitive-level phone floors (min 44px, 16px inputs), scrolling tab chips.
- [P1] Dialogs are centred modals with a 15px close; three modal systems. Fix: shared Dialog becomes a bottom sheet below md with a 44px close.
- [P1] Header bloat: stats/eyebrows/heroes push data 300-600px down (students, classes/[id], spreadsheets, payroll, clinic). Fix: action row first, stats as one compact strip, hide all-zero stats, delete eyebrows.
- [P1] Owner's second tab (Payments) opens gateway secret keys, not money in.
